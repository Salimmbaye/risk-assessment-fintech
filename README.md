# Information Security Risk Assessment (NIST CSF 2.0)

An end-to-end information security risk assessment for a fictional fintech, delivered as
an **Excel workbook** (live scoring + heatmap + dashboard) and a **Word report**.

> Portfolio / lab project. *NorthStar Lending* is fictional; all data is invented.

## What's here
- **`Risk-Assessment-NIST-CSF.xlsx`** — the working analysis:
  - **Risk Register** — 14 risks scored with live formulas (Likelihood × Impact), inherent
    and residual, color-coded by band.
  - **Risk Heatmap** — a 5×5 grid counting residual risks, colored Low→Critical.
  - **Dashboard** — counts by risk level and NIST CSF function, with a chart.
  - **Quantitative (SLE-ALE)** — an annualized-loss model translating a top risk into dollars.
- **`Risk-Assessment-Report.docx`** — the written report: company scenario, methodology
  (NIST SP 800-30), findings, the top-4 critical risks, and a remediation roadmap.

## Method
Semi-quantitative scoring (Risk = Likelihood × Impact, 1–5), assessing **inherent** vs.
**residual** risk, with a quantitative loss layer (SLE × ARO = ALE) for the top risks.

## Skills demonstrated
Risk assessment & risk registers · NIST CSF 2.0 · NIST SP 800-30 · inherent vs. residual
risk · quantitative risk (SLE/ALE) · Excel formulas, conditional formatting & dashboards.

## Standards referenced
NIST CSF 2.0 · NIST SP 800-30 · FTC Safeguards Rule (GLBA)
